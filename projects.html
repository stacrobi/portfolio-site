Project Two

Respect, Civility & Inclusion Training Program

Instructor-Led Enterprise Learning Experience (ILT + Facilitator Enablement)

  • Problem: A large public-sector organization needed a scalable, policy-aligned training experience to strengthen workplace conduct expectations, reinforce respectful workplace culture, and equip employees and supervisors to recognize, respond to, and report inappropriate workplace behavior. The organization required a solution that could support psychologically safe conversations while translating complex workplace policies into practical, real-world employee behaviors.
  • Solution: Designed and developed a highly interactive instructor-led training program that included:
    • Enterprise ILT slide deck
    • Detailed Facilitator Guide
    • Participant Guide
    • Scenario-based activities
    • Role-play exercises
    • Psychological safety facilitation strategies
    • Policy-aligned reporting workflows
    • Interactive discussion architecture for mixed employee audiences
    The experience was intentionally designed to move beyond compliance lecture delivery and instead drive discussion, reflection, behavioral judgment, and workplace application.
  • Results: Created a scalable facilitation system capable of supporting consistent delivery across multiple MWRD locations and varying room configurations while improving facilitator readiness, learner engagement, and practical policy application.

Project Three

AI-Assisted Compliance Training Video Development

A scenario-based compliance training video created to support workplace learning on harassment, discrimination, and retaliation while demonstrating how AI can be used strategically across script development, visual production, and animation workflow.

  • Problem: The organization needed a realistic, policy-aligned training video for harassment, discrimination, and retaliation education, but traditional video production would have required more time, coordination, and budget than the project allowed.
  • Solution: Designed a streamlined AI-assisted production workflow by using Claude to draft the video script, Manus to generate the visual assets, and Google Flow to animate those assets into a finished training video. The workflow reduced production friction while preserving instructional intent, tone, and policy alignment.
  • Results: Produced a usable digital learning asset that supported scenario-based compliance training, accelerated development, and demonstrated how AI could be applied strategically across scripting, visual production, and media assembly.

Tools Used: Claude, Manus, Google Flow

Project Seven

Reducing LGBTQ+ Barriers to Care Through Online Teammate Education

A research-based healthcare learning initiative created to strengthen inclusive care practices, increase teammate confidence, and improve cultural awareness across Atrium Health.

  • Problem: Research identified that LGBTQ+ patients frequently experience discrimination and disparities within healthcare settings, leading to delayed care, avoidance of preventative services, and poorer health outcomes. Atrium Health recognized the need to improve teammate education, cultural awareness, and inclusive care practices across the organization.
  • Solution: Partnered with physician leaders and stakeholders at Atrium Health to design and develop a twenty minute interactive eLearning module focused on LGBTQ+ patient populations, inclusive communication, healthcare disparities, and culturally responsive care practices. The course incorporated videos, knowledge checks, and reflective learning activities to strengthen teammate understanding and comfort levels when caring for LGBTQ+ patients.
  • Results: The eLearning module was successfully launched across Atrium Health and contributed to measurable increases in teammate comfort, awareness, and knowledge related to LGBTQ+ patient care. The initiative was later featured as a conference poster presentation and awarded second place recognition for its impact and contribution to teammate education and inclusive healthcare practices.
